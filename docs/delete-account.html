Delete Your MentoMate Account

Last updated: July 2026

MentoMate, operated by ZWIZZLY AS, lets account owners request deletion either inside the app or by email. You do not need to reinstall the app to use the email option.

Delete in the app

  1. Sign in to MentoMate.
  2. Open More, then Privacy & Data.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Review the warning, type DELETE, and confirm.

Request deletion without the app

Email us from the address associated with your MentoMate account. Use the subject “Delete my MentoMate account”. If you cannot send from that address, include the account email so we can verify that you are authorised to make the request. Never send your password or learning content.

Email privacy@mentomate.com

We will verify the request before scheduling deletion.

What happens next

Account deletion has a 7-day grace period. During that period, you may cancel the deletion from MentoMate. After the grace period, the account and associated personal and learning data are permanently removed, except for limited records we must retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting reasons.

Deleting your MentoMate account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel an active subscription separately in Google Play before deleting the account to prevent future renewal.
