## Full description

```text
MentoMate is an AI-guided learning app for learners aged 13+ and adults. Work through school subjects with guided questions, explanations, practice, and review.

Learn with your Mentor
• Ask a question or start a guided learning session
• Type, use voice transcription, or add a homework photo
• Practise ideas step by step instead of only receiving an answer
• Return to subjects, notes, and saved learning in one place

Organise your learning
• Create and manage subjects
• Review progress and learning activity
• Save useful Mentor replies and notes in your Journal
• Use optional reminders to return to learning

For families
• Adult account owners can create linked learner profiles
• Parents can review linked learners’ activity and progress
• Account owners can export data and schedule account deletion in the app

MentoMate requires an account. Camera and photo access are used only when you choose homework-image features. Microphone access supports voice transcription. Mobile subscriptions, when available, use Google Play billing.
```

## Copy guardrails

The following claims from the historical store-description draft are
deliberately excluded:

- “COPPA/GDPR compliant”;
- guaranteed or “optimal” learning/memory outcomes;
- “learn any subject” as an unqualified coverage promise;
- under-16 parental-consent claims that ignore country rules;
- public Family/Pro availability before store products are confirmed;
- on-device-only audio or no-retention claims not established by evidence;
- school, classroom, LMS/SIS, formal assessment, proctoring, mental-health, or
  emotional-support positioning.
