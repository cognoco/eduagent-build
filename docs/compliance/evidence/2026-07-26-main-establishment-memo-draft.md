# Memo — Main Establishment and Competent Supervisory Authority

**Company:** ZWIZZLY AS, org.nr 811 696 072, registered in Oslo, Norway
**Prepared:** 2026-07-26 (draft for management verification and signature)
**Purpose:** Records the determination of the company's main establishment (GDPR Article 4(16)) and its competent/lead supervisory authority (Articles 55–56), as requested in the DPO Interim DPIA Advice Record (action 2).

## 1. Corporate structure

ZWIZZLY AS is a single Norwegian limited company (aksjeselskap) registered in the Register of Business Enterprises (Foretaksregisteret), org.nr 811 696 072, with its registered office in Oslo. Evidence: company registration certificate (provided to the DPO 2026-07-24).

The company has **no subsidiaries, branches, or other establishments** in any other country, and no other office, registered presence, or management location anywhere (confirmed by management 2026-07-26).

**Trade name.** The company operates its product under the consumer brand **MentoMate**. It is not a separate legal entity; all processing is carried out by ZWIZZLY AS alone.

## 2. Where processing decisions are made

Decisions on the **purposes and means of processing** — what personal data the MentoMate service collects, why, with which providers, and under which safeguards — are made exclusively by the company's management in Norway. The Chair of the Board, Zuzana Kopecna, is the decision-maker for all data-protection matters and signs all controller documentation (DPIA, RoPA, provider agreements, this memo).

There is no other place of central administration and no establishment elsewhere with decision-making power over processing.

## 3. Determination

- **Main establishment (Art 4(16)(a)):** the place of central administration — **Oslo, Norway**. As the company has only one establishment, no competing analysis arises.
- **Competent supervisory authority (Art 55):** **Datatilsynet** (the Norwegian Data Protection Authority), as the authority of the member state of establishment. Norway applies the GDPR through its incorporation into the EEA Agreement and the Norwegian Personal Data Act (personopplysningsloven, 2018).
- **Lead supervisory authority / one-stop-shop (Art 56):** the service processes data of users resident in several EEA countries (cross-border processing, Art 4(23)(b)). With a single establishment in Norway, **Datatilsynet is the lead supervisory authority** for that cross-border processing under the one-stop-shop mechanism, which extends to Norway via the EEA Agreement (with the standard EEA-institutional caveats regarding EDPB participation).

## 4. Consequences

- DPIA prior consultation (Art 36), if ever required, is addressed to Datatilsynet.
- Personal-data breach notifications (Art 33) go to Datatilsynet.
- The DPO's contact details are communicated to Datatilsynet (Art 37(7)) — **pending**: to be filed only after the formal DPO appointment is signed and the dpo@ mailbox is live, per the DPO's own instruction not to register before appointment.
- Users anywhere may still complain to their local authority (Art 77); such cases would be coordinated toward Datatilsynet as lead where the one-stop-shop applies.

## 5. Revisit triggers

This determination is re-examined if the company: opens an office or establishment outside Norway; moves central administration; creates any subsidiary; or restructures decision-making so that purposes/means decisions are made elsewhere.
