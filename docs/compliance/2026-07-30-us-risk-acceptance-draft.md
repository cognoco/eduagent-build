## 3. What management is accepting

By signing, management accepts the residual US legal risk described in the screen record and the deep dive, specifically:

- The screen's GDPR/Article-8 reasoning (13-year-old self-consent for US residents, per the DPO's non-EEA reading) is a **reasoned interpretation**, not settled law.
- Texas SB 2420 is **already in effect**; our compliance rests on the store-provided signal handling under WI-1116, not on a fully-verified integration as of this draft.
- The companion-chatbot laws (CA SB 243, NY GBL Art 47) carry **no revenue/user thresholds** — our "out of scope" position rests on persistent memory being off at launch, and that position **reverses** the day knows-me ships, exposing CA statutory damages ($1,000/violation, private right of action) and NY AG penalties (up to $15,000/day) if the re-assessment trigger (Section 4, item 8) is missed.
- KOSA/KIDS Act, Colorado AI Act, and the state AADCs (CA, SC, Vermont) are assessed as not-yet-applicable or below-threshold, but several convert to "applicable" on fixed future dates or user-count milestones tracked in Section 4.
- No geo-exclusion of individual US states (e.g., CA, NY) is currently possible — our residence gating operates per-country only.

9. **Any US marketing copy implying emotional support** → immediate compliance review (standing constraint: never position MentoMate as mental-health or emotional-wellbeing support in the US).
