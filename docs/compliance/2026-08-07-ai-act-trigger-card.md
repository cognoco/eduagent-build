# AI Act Trigger Card — carry into every commercial conversation

**One page. Plain language. If any line below is about to become true — stop, say you need to check
something, and end the topic. No agreement, pilot, letter of intent, or "sure, we can explore that" until
a new classification assessment is approved.**

This card implements measure M2 of the
[Article 4 AI-literacy note](2026-07-30-ai-act-art4-ai-literacy-note.md). The authoritative wording of
every trigger is the classification record
([`2026-07-30-eu-ai-act-classification-record.md`](2026-07-30-eu-ai-act-classification-record.md) §7);
this card is the field version, not a substitute.

---

## The seven stop-signs

| # | Stop if… | Sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A **school, university, training provider, tutoring company, or public authority** wants to require, deploy, or even *recommend* MentoMate as part of how they teach | "Could our district roll this out?" · "We'd recommend it to our students" |
| 2 | **Teachers or institutions would rely on MentoMate's outputs** to grade, place, promote, certify, or steer formal learning — arranged, integrated, marketed, or contracted by us | "Can the teacher see the mastery scores?" · "Export progress to our gradebook" *(a learner voluntarily using their own results in schoolwork is fine)* |
| 3 | Anyone proposes **integration with an LMS, SIS, gradebook, exam system, or official student record** | "Do you have a Canvas/Teams/Itslearning integration?" |
| 4 | **Mastery or profiling data would be used in any institutional setting** | "We just need the analytics side for our teachers" |
| 5 | Anyone proposes **exam monitoring, cheating detection, biometrics, emotion or affect detection** | "Can it tell when the student is frustrated?" · "Proctoring mode?" |
| 6 | We would **materially change the intended purpose, audience, our provider role, the underlying models, or public marketing claims** | A new audience, a new model provider, a repositioned landing page |
| 7 | We would **build or market our own general-purpose AI model** instead of integrating third-party models | "Why not train our own model?" |

## Words we never use in public copy

school-ready · classroom-ready · institution-ready · "approved under the EU AI Act" · regulator-approved ·
suitable for formal assessment · grading · proctoring support

(Full allowed/blocked vocabulary:
[`2026-07-06-school-institutional-ai-act-tripwire.md`](2026-07-06-school-institutional-ai-act-tripwire.md).)

## If a stop-sign comes up

1. Don't agree to anything, however small or exploratory.
2. Note what was asked, by whom, in one sentence.
3. Route it: reassessment before any yes — owner: Zuzana Kopečná (classification record §9); escalation:
   external DPO.

*Why this card exists: MentoMate is **not high-risk** under the EU AI Act only because it is a voluntary,
family-purchased consumer product whose outputs decide nothing outside the app. Every line above is a way
that stops being true.*
