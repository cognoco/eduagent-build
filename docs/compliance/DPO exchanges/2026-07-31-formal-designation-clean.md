## Designation

ZWIZZLY AS formally designates Stephan Hartmann as its external Data Protection Officer under Articles 37-39 GDPR and the applicable Norwegian data-protection framework. Stephan Hartmann accepts the designation subject to the External Data Protection Officer Services Agreement.

## Management confirmation

ZWIZZLY AS confirms that the External DPO will report directly to the highest management level, will be involved properly and in a timely manner in all personal-data matters and will have access to the information, personnel, systems and resources reasonably required to perform the role.

ZWIZZLY AS further confirms that the External DPO will act independently, will not receive instructions concerning the performance or conclusions of statutory DPO tasks and will not be dismissed or penalised for performing those tasks.

## Tasks

The designation covers the tasks stated in Article 39 GDPR, including informing and advising the Company and its personnel, monitoring compliance, advising on and monitoring DPIAs, cooperating with supervisory authorities and acting as a contact point for authorities and data subjects. The role will be performed using a risk-based approach.
