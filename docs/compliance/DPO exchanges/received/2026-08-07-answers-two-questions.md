# Received: DPO answers to the two bounded questions (2026-08-07)

## 1. Memory-unlock closure set

Your proposed reading is close, but I would add the memory-relevant elements of Action 5. The evidence package for lifting the specific interim condition on persistent memory and profiling should cover:

- Action 3, insofar as it concerns residence determination, age assurance and the controls governing which users may access the memory function;
- Action 4, including the specific purposes and legal bases for memory and profiling, the consent and withdrawal design where consent is relied upon, the relevant records and the consequences of withdrawal;
- the memory-relevant elements of Action 5, particularly the Article 9 position and evidence that sensitive or safeguarding-related content is suppressed or excluded from persistent memory where required;
- Action 6, including retention and deletion rules for raw content, derived profiles, summaries, embeddings, backups and any provider-held copies; and
- the memory-specific elements of Action 13, particularly the child-facing explanation, interface disclosures and transparency concerning what is remembered, why it is remembered, how it affects the service and how the user can inspect, correct, disable or delete it.

Action 5 should therefore not be omitted. The current statement in the register that closing Actions 3, 4 and 6 is the unlock should be revised to refer to the relevant parts of Actions 3, 4, 5, 6 and 13.

I agree that the complete broader Article 35(9) consultation or testing programme does not have to be treated as part of this narrower feature-specific closure set, except to the extent that proportionate testing is needed to demonstrate that the memory disclosures are understandable to the intended child users. The remaining broader testing work may continue to be tracked as a separate launch item.

This confirmation concerns only the specific memory/profiling operating condition. It does not override other launch gates. In particular:

- any provider, model, endpoint, embedding service or storage recipient used for memory must also satisfy the applicable Actions 7–10 before receiving production learner data;
- any guardian visibility into remembered information would engage the relevant elements of Action 12; and
- the public launch remains subject to the consolidated DPIA and the remaining applicable launch conditions.

You may now assemble the memory-specific evidence package on that basis and send it for review.
